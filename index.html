Where the money slips away

Every manufacturer has its own rules, windows and forms. When one person handles claims for a dozen of them alongside everything else, some credits never come back.

  • Claims never filed

    Eligible sales that didn't match an agreement in the system, so no claim went out.

  • Rejections nobody chased

    A wrong contract number or missing detail, and the claim quietly dies.

  • Short payments

    Credits that came back smaller than what was claimed, without anyone noticing.

  • Wrong price on the invoice

    Orders billed at list price because a quote number was missing from the PO.

  • Expired agreements

    Sales made after an SPA lapsed or before it was loaded, and never sorted out.

  • Missed windows

    Claims that were valid but filed too late for the manufacturer to accept.

How a claim check works

We start small, with one manufacturer, so you can see results before committing to anything.

  1. You send a small sample

    About 90 days of claims, rejections and credits for one manufacturer, plus the matching agreements. Customer names can be removed first.

  2. We match every eligible sale

    Each sale is checked against your agreements, what you claimed, and what was actually paid.

  3. You get a recovery list

    Every credit you can still claim, with the supporting evidence and the filing deadline for each one.

  4. Your team files the claims

    You stay in control of your manufacturer relationships. We never log into your portals.
